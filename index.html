About me

My interests span web development, machine learning and AI.

Before software engineering, I worked as a data scientist at the University of Michigan, where I designed and trained multilevel statistical models in Python, R and SAS. That work contributed to research published in Science Advances. Today I train and evaluate large language models, reviewing code and reasoning to make AI more accurate.

As a full-stack engineer, I have worked with JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Python, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, HTML, CSS, Ruby on Rails.

Outside of coding, I make films: I shoot and edit videos and fly my drone when I travel. I also dance. Ballet was a big part of my life for 8 years, and now I focus on house and contemporary.

Airbcb

Airbcb, short for air bed, car & breakfast, is a clone of Airbnb inspired by the movie "The Holiday", where users can lease both their apartment and their car for short-term stays. Built with React and Redux on the front end and a Ruby on Rails API backed by a PostgreSQL database. Listing photos are stored on AWS S3 and locations are shown with the Google Maps API. Users can sign up, log in, search listings and leave reviews.

    GitHubGitHub Live Site

The goal is to make it to the finish line defeating enemies and gaining a higher possible score along the way.
